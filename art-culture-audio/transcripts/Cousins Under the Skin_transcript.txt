The Bronx Zoo view of human evolution was once widespread. What seems like the simple explanation that dark skin protects against heat is wrong. Without vitamin D, children get rickets, their bones are soft and deform easily. Ancient graves show that this has been a problem for thousands of years. Under UV light, whites synthesize a useful dose of vitamin D in only half an hour, while blacks take three hours to do so. Natural selection acted to favour those with genes for relatively light skins as humanity began its long walk from the tropics to the dismal climates of the north.
